# Key Claims and Explicit Non-Claims

## Claims directly supported by the executed evidence

- The implemented forecasting pipeline uses only causal past input windows and reconstructs from the **observed meter at t-1**.
- In the exact 10-seed encoder-matched head-replacement experiment, the PSNN-headed DOptDRPSNN has substantially lower RMSE variance than the matched DRA-GRU head, while DRA-GRU has lower mean RMSE.
- The final anomaly-calibration head transfers to the held-out buildings with measurable discrimination, and removing PSNN spike rate reduces unseen-building anomaly performance.
- The sequential MOA->DOA pipeline uses a 9/15-feature input vector and shows a modest optimizer-role benefit under the reported reduced-budget ablation; MOA is retained primarily for compactness/search decomposition.
- The supervisory-control result is generated by a controller that reads the model forecast and anomaly probability; the measured effect is small and bounded.

## Claims deliberately not made

- DOptDRPSNN is **not** claimed to be the most accurate one-hour or six-hour forecaster.
- PSNN is **not** claimed to dominate GRU/LSTM/Transformer in mean forecasting accuracy or missing-input RMSE.
- MOA is **not** claimed to find a universally accuracy-optimal feature mask.
- The short synthetic control experiment is **not** claimed to establish large real-building savings, annual savings, or peak-demand reduction.
- No anomaly result is attributed to ASHRAE/BDG2, which do not provide the controlled clean counterfactual/fault labels used here.
- No unexecuted ASHRAE/BDG2 numerical result is presented as experimental evidence.
